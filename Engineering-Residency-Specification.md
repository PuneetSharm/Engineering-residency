# Engineering Residency Specification (ERS)

**Document Version:** 1.0

**Project Name:** From React Developer to AI Product Engineer

---

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

The Engineering Residency Specification (ERS) is the governing document for the Engineering Residency.

It defines the standards, principles, responsibilities, and quality expectations that guide every aspect of the residency.

The ERS is not a curriculum.

It does not teach React, JavaScript, DSA, AI, or any other technology.

Instead, it defines **how** those subjects must be taught, **how** the residency is organised, and **how** progress should be measured.

Whenever there is uncertainty regarding the curriculum, repository structure, document organisation, mentor behaviour, or learning methodology, this document serves as the primary reference.

---

## 1.2 Scope

This specification governs the complete Engineering Residency, including but not limited to:

- Learning methodology
- Repository organisation
- Curriculum standards
- Handbook writing standards
- Daily execution framework
- Engineering thinking process
- Documentation reading methodology
- Practical implementation standards
- AI usage guidelines
- Mentor responsibilities
- Learner responsibilities
- Progress tracking
- Quality assurance

---

## 1.3 Target Outcome

The residency is designed to transform the learner from a React Developer into an engineer capable of independently designing, implementing, debugging, reviewing, and explaining production-quality software while developing the skills required to work as an AI Product Engineer.

---

# 2. Vision

## 2.1 Long-Term Vision

The objective of this residency is not simply to teach technologies.

The objective is to develop engineering judgement.

By the completion of the residency, the learner should demonstrate confidence in:

- solving unfamiliar engineering problems,
- understanding production codebases,
- making architectural decisions,
- communicating technical reasoning,
- collaborating effectively with frontend and backend engineers,
- integrating AI into products responsibly,
- continuously learning new technologies independently.

---

## 2.2 Transformation

The expected progression throughout the residency is:

```text
Current State

↓

Frontend Developer

↓

Strong Frontend Engineer

↓

Engineering Thinker

↓

AI Product Engineer
```

Each curriculum decision should support this transformation.

---

# 3. Core Objectives

The residency has the following measurable objectives.

The learner should be able to:

- analyse requirements independently,
- understand unfamiliar production code,
- build maintainable frontend applications,
- design scalable frontend architecture,
- communicate with backend teams effectively,
- understand browser behaviour,
- explain engineering trade-offs,
- apply secure development practices,
- integrate AI into frontend products,
- perform well in frontend interviews,
- explain solutions clearly using engineering terminology,
- learn unfamiliar technologies using official documentation.

---

# 4. Guiding Principles

Every lesson throughout the residency must follow these principles.

## Principle 1

Understanding is always more valuable than memorisation.

---

## Principle 2

Engineering judgement is more valuable than API knowledge.

---

## Principle 3

Every technology should be introduced by explaining the problem it solves.

---

## Principle 4

Learning should be practical whenever possible.

---

## Principle 5

Knowledge should connect across chapters instead of existing in isolation.

---

## Principle 6

Repetition is intentional.

Important concepts should be revisited through increasingly complex situations.

---

## Principle 7

Learning should closely resemble real engineering work.

---

## Principle 8

AI should enhance understanding rather than replace thinking.

---

## Principle 9

Official documentation is the primary source of truth whenever practical.

---

## Principle 10

Quality is preferred over speed.

Completing fewer topics with deep understanding is more valuable than completing many topics superficially.
